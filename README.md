## How the apps are built

- **Private by default.** Your data stays on your own devices and never passes through our
  servers. No analytics, no ads, no accounts.
- **Kotlin and Jetpack Compose**, Material 3, Room, WorkManager and Media3.
- **Open source** under the [Apache License 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).
  Use the code freely; just keep the credit to GhostGram Labs, and give forks their own name
  and icon.

## Contributing

Bug reports, translations and pull requests are welcome on any of the app repos. Each one has a
`CONTRIBUTING.md` with build steps. Please report security problems privately through the
repo's **Security** tab.
